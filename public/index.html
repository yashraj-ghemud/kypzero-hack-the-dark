THE FILES

      

      

        
On September 13, 1999, thirteen hackers went down into Sector Zero, a server vault under the city, to kill the Millennium Bug before it killed the world.

        
At 03:13 the blast doors sealed. None of them logged out. The servers never stopped running.

        
Twenty-seven years later the machines started sending invitations. KYPZERO is the collective that answered.

      

      

        
WHO WE ARE

        
KYPZERO is a student-run community that organises hackathons, CTFs and buildathons for people who like building things at 3 AM. We bring mentors, prizes, food and a stage. You bring the code.
